Why “e-Drift”?

Threats often arrive as small compromises in routine — quiet moments where a habit drifts. Security e-Drift names those moments early, while the fix is still ordinary. The tagline on the logo — the quiet compromise — is the thing worth catching while the fix is still ordinary.
