The quiet compromise

Most harm does not arrive as a movie hack. It arrives as a small drift in routine: a rushed click, a shared password, a friendly supplier code that never got revoked, a bot promoted from “help me think” to “just send it.”

Remember this: name the compromise early, and the week stays ordinary.
