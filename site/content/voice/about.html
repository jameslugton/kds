Why “e‑Drift”?

Most threats don’t arrive as drama — they arrive as drift. A quiet compromise in a routine. A habit that slips. A moment where someone is tired, rushed, or distracted by a child asking for a snack.

Security e‑Drift names those moments early, while the fix is still ordinary. The tagline — the quiet compromise — is the thing worth catching while the fix is still ordinary.
