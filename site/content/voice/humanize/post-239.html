Cybersecurity is not reserved for degrees, certifications, or job titles. Parents, freelancers, shop owners, students, and grandparents all make security decisions. The useful skill is ordinary: a pause to ask whether a message, link, or request is real before you act. Useful habits • You ask questions before clicking. • You share tips with colleagues, friends, and family. • You keep your devices updated. • You learn from mistakes—and from what you read, watch, and hear. • You run active antivirus and use multi-factor authentication. • You speak up when something feels off. That’s not boring. That’s powerful. That’s leadership in action. Keeping it ordinary You don’t need to be perfect. You just need to be present. Every time you take a small step. checking a link, updating a password, warning a friend you’re shaping a safer digital world. You’re not just protecting yourself. You’re becoming someone others look to. Someone who leads by example.
