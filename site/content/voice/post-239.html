Most security work is not dramatic. It is the pause before you click, the password you change before you are told to, the odd message you mention to a colleague instead of quietly hoping it was fine.

No cape required — just habits you can keep on an ordinary Tuesday.

Habits that actually help

  • Ask questions before you click
  • Keep devices updated
  • Use unique passwords — and an extra step to guard important accounts with multi-factor authentication (mfa)
  • Say something when a message feels off — at home or at work
  • Share a real scam warning with someone who might get the same bait

At the moment of risk

Stop! Pause! Before you click the link: Is it urgent? Do you know who it is from? Is it really from who it is from? If the answer is not sure or no — do not click. Check another way.

A self-check

Look at one account you care about — email or banking. Is the password something only you know, and long enough that guessing it would be hard? Have you turned on that extra phone check (mfa)? If not, that is this week’s job — one account is enough to start.
