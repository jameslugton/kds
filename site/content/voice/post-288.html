Cybersecurity often sounds like specialist tools. Day to day, a short habit usually matters more: a few minutes to glance at devices, inbox, and accounts — the way you check the door is locked.

Here is a simple 10-minute routine. Keep what fits. Skip what does not.

1. Device glance (2 minutes)

Quick look — not a deep dive:

  • Unexpected pop-ups?
  • Antivirus still running?
  • Updates waiting?
  • Device unusually slow or hot?

You are noticing, not fixing everything at once. Early notice is the point.

2. Inbox sweep (3 minutes)

Scan for:

  • Login or password-reset alerts you did not trigger
  • “Urgent” messages that do not feel right
  • Attachments from unknown senders
  • Supplier or colleague emails that look slightly off

Stop! Pause! Before you click: Is it urgent? Do you know who it is from? Is it really from them? Not sure — do not click. Check another channel.

3. One small account job (3–5 minutes)

Pick one:

  • Update one old or short password
  • Add an extra step to guard one account with multi-factor authentication (mfa)
  • Remove an unused login or app connection
  • Check recent logins on email or banking

A self-check

What is the passcode on your bank app — and when did you last change it? If it is short, reused somewhere else, or something a stranger could guess from your social posts, change that one today.
