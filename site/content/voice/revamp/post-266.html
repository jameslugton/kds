You are on a road trip. Someone thumbs a lift. They seem fine. You chat. Miles later you stop for fuel, and they already know where you keep the spare key.

A lot of digital risk looks like that hitchhiker: a browser extension that “helps,” a contractor login that was never removed, a shared password in a chat thread, a plugin installed for one weekend and left forever.

Trust is not the problem. Unreviewed access is.

The habit

  • Give people and tools the least they need
  • Put an end date on temporary accounts
  • Review who can see the mailbox and the accounts folder every so often — especially after someone leaves
  • If something goes wrong, revoke first, tidy later

Politeness should not keep a stranger in the passenger seat.

A self-check

Who still has access to something important — an old colleague, a contractor, a browser extension you forgot about? Pick one and revoke or review it today. Hitchhikers leave when you ask them to.
