# Security e-Drift — niche product positioning

## Category

Calm cybersecurity for UK homes and small teams.

A readable field guide for people who make real decisions under ordinary pressure — not threat theatre, and not enterprise compliance theatre.

## Promise

**Stay calm. Stay sharp. Spot the quiet compromise while the fix is still ordinary.**

## Who it is for

- Household readers who want to spot scams without panic
- Freelancers and micro-business owners who need baselines that fit a Tuesday
- Small teams adopting AI who need human brakes, not buzzwords

## Who it is not for

- People hunting CVE dumps or SOC dashboards
- Fear-first “you’re already doomed” content
- Generic affiliate-heavy cybersecurity SEO blogs

## Signature products (owned language)

| Product | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **The quiet compromise** | Name the small routine drift that creates risk |
| **Advisor vs actor** | Decide what AI is allowed to *do* |
| **Jim’s shop** | Learn through short story chapters |
| **SMB checklist 2026** | One printable baseline |
| **AI policy template** | Paste rules + human confirms |

## Brand voice

Calm, specific, UK-plain. Prefer one memorable rule over ten tips. Never mock the reader. Never sell fear.

## Competitive contrast

| Typical cyber blog | Security e-Drift |
| --- | --- |
| Urgency and dread | Steady tone |
| Tool roundups | Habits and judgement |
| US enterprise framing | UK home + small team |
| Infinite tips | Short paths + next action |
